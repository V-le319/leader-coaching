    body: "A script isn't about sounding robotic — it's a starting point so you're not improvising the hardest sentence of the conversation in real time. Once you've said it once, it gets easier to make it your own."
  - heading: "Four conversations, four starting points"
    body: "Performance is slipping — lead with the specific pattern, not a general impression, and ask before assuming. Someone needs to hear they're doing well — specificity is what makes praise land instead of feeling generic. A behavior needs to stop — be direct, specific, and tied to a real consequence. Someone disagrees with your decision — acknowledge the disagreement without re-opening the whole decision."
mistakes:
  - "Burying the point in a compliment sandwich. People remember the compliments and forget the actual issue."
  - "Waiting for the \"right moment.\" The right moment is usually just after the thing happens, not weeks later."
  - "Softening it into vagueness. \"Just something to think about\" doesn't tell anyone what to actually change."
