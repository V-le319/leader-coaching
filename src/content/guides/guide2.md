Most leadership skills aren't complicated — they're just unfamiliar. This mini-course breaks the basics into eight short lessons, one a day, so you can build the habit without blocking out hours you don't have.

## How the 8 days work

Each day covers one specific skill, with a short read and a single action to try before the next lesson. There's no homework pile-up — if you miss a day, just pick up where you left off.

### Day 1–2: Understanding the shift
What actually changes when you go from doing the work to leading the people doing the work.

### Day 3–4: Delegation basics
How to hand off a task without it boomeranging back to you a week later.

### Day 5–6: Running better 1:1s
Turning a status-update meeting into a conversation that actually builds trust.

### Day 7–8: Giving feedback early
Saying the small thing before it becomes the big thing.

## What people get out of it

- **A daily rhythm.** Ten minutes a day is easier to sustain than a single long workshop.
- **Real, small actions.** Each lesson ends with one thing to actually try — not just something to think about.
- **A foundation for everything else.** This mini-course feeds directly into the New Manager Bootcamp if you want to go deeper afterward.

## Getting started

Sign up once, and a new lesson lands in your inbox each morning for eight days. Nothing to schedule, nothing to prepare — just eight small steps toward feeling less like you're guessing.
